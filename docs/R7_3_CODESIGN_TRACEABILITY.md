## Interpretation and report text

The June 2024 Slido records provide contemporaneous evidence of participatory requirements elicitation. Suggestions addressed public maps and results, sample identity and personal access, automatic field/laboratory integration, photos, research data access, interactive analysis and external data sources. A retrospective crosswalk demonstrates strong correspondence between a substantial subset of these themes and later validated ECHOREPO capabilities. Open-ended proposals also identify unresolved or differently scoped needs, including social interaction, incentive mechanisms, advanced comparative interpretation and mobile collection. The evidence is complemented by documented later user feedback (issue #4) and current usability evaluation. This is evidence of an iterative participatory design process, not proof of causal implementation history, representativeness or attainment of the satisfaction target.

R73-I04 remains PARTIAL with stronger documentary support: dated co-design input and later matching functionality are now evidenced, but formal decisions/causal implementation history and additional workshop records remain incomplete. The current satisfaction survey is separate.
