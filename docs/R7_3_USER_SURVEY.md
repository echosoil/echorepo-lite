# R7-3 / WI5-3 — Proposed anonymous ECHOREPO user survey

**Status:** Draft for project/ethics review; not yet administered. No satisfaction result is available.

## Purpose and participants

Evaluate actual experience with ECHOREPO (web portal, search/map, data export/API and research workspace where used), not the mobile ECHOsoil app unless separately identified. Invite all reasonably reachable eligible users across relevant roles and countries, not only favorable contacts. Participation is voluntary. Record recruitment method, survey dates, eligible invitation count if known, respondent count and limitations; a convenience sample is not representative by default.

Use a project-approved survey service configured without sign-in, email collection, account identifiers, or collection of IP addresses where configurable; verify platform logging and privacy notice before describing the survey as anonymous. Do not request names, organizations, sample identifiers, location or private data. Limit access, retain only necessary information and follow the consortium's approved retention and consent procedures. Warn respondents not to disclose identifying details in free text. Use aggregate results in reports; avoid small identifying subgroup breakdowns.

## Suggested form

Introduction: This short voluntary questionnaire evaluates ECHOREPO for the ECHO project. Please answer based on your actual use. Responses are analyzed in aggregate for software quality and user experience evaluation. Do not include personal information in any comment. Participation is optional and does not affect access to project services. Provide the approved data-handling notice/contact before launch.

1. Have you used ECHOREPO (the soil data web repository, not just the mobile app)? Yes / No / Not sure. Only Yes respondents continue to experience questions.
2. Which functions have you used? (Multiple selection; optional.) Map/explore; search/filter; data download; API; personal dashboard; Jupyter/Streamlit research tools; other.
3. Overall, how satisfied are you with ECHOREPO? Very dissatisfied (1), dissatisfied (2), neither satisfied nor dissatisfied (3), satisfied (4), very satisfied (5), unable to assess.
4. How easy was it to find the information or data you needed? 1 very difficult – 5 very easy; not applicable.
5. How satisfied were you with the response speed? 1 very dissatisfied – 5 very satisfied; not applicable.
6. How clear and understandable were the interface and data descriptions? 1 very unclear – 5 very clear; not applicable.
7. How satisfied were you with the available functions for your intended tasks? 1 very dissatisfied – 5 very satisfied; not applicable.
8. Did you encounter a problem that prevented you from completing a task? Yes / No / Not applicable.
9. What is the single most useful improvement we could make? Optional free text; do not include identifying or confidential information.

## Predefined indicator

Primary metric: percentage of eligible respondents giving an overall satisfaction rating of 4 or 5 on question 3. Denominator: all eligible respondents giving a valid 1–5 answer to Q3. Exclude non-users, missing answers and unable-to-assess responses from that denominator, but report their counts. Count each eligible response once under a documented duplicate-handling rule; do not introduce identifying tracking solely for deduplication. If no valid responses, report not assessed, not zero or 100%.

Formula: 100 × (number of Q3 ratings 4 or 5) / (number of valid Q3 ratings 1–5).

WP2 suggested a target of at least 80%; confirm it before launch. Report numerator, denominator, all five rating counts and uncertainty/sample-selection limitations. Report response rate only if eligible invitation denominator is known. Do not substitute an average of other questions, a test-pass rate, or usability observations for this satisfaction indicator. Agree a practical recruitment goal in advance and report small samples explicitly; no fabricated responses or selective exclusion.

## Co-design evidence

If existing workshops cannot be documented, conduct a short new feedback session with consenting users: typical tasks (find a soil sample, apply a filter, interpret/download data where authorized), observed difficulties, proposed improvements and agreed actions. Record date, participant count, tasks and anonymized findings; track resulting changes or dispositions. Do not describe the new session as an earlier historical workshop.

## Evidence to retain

Approved questionnaire/version, privacy notice, dates and recruitment details, aggregate response counts, anonymized summary of issues, and a dated analysis report. Keep raw responses in approved controlled storage; publish only reviewed aggregate results.
